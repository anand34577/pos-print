# Getting Started

This takes about ten minutes once the app is installed. You need a thermal printer that is switched on and has paper.

### 1. Pair the printer (Bluetooth)

Open the app and add a printer from the Printers tab. Allow the Bluetooth permission when asked, then pick your printer from the scan list. If pairing asks for a PIN, try `0000` or `1234`.

You should see the printer in the list with a status chip showing it is connected.

### 2. Or connect over USB

Plug the printer into the phone with a data-capable OTG cable and tap **Allow** on the system prompt. If you dismissed it, unplug the cable and plug it back in.

### 3. Choose the printer type

In the setup step, pick the paper width (58 or 80 mm) and the language: ESC/POS for receipt printers, or TSPL, ZPL, EPL or CPCL for label printers.

### 4. Send a test print

Use **Test print**. You should get a page with a ruler line and some text. If the ruler wraps or is cut off, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).

### 5. Fill in your business details

Open Settings and add your business name, address, phone, tax ID, footer and logo. Templates read these through `{{business.name}}` and the other business values.

### 6. Print a receipt

Go to Templates, open one of the ten starters, check the live preview and tap **Print**.
