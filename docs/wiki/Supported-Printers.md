# Supported Printers

* **Receipt printers:** any ESC/POS 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer over Bluetooth SPP or USB. Epson TM series, Xprinter, Rongta, Gprinter, Zjiang, Munbyn, Sunmi and generic clones should work.
* **Label printers:** TSPL (TSC, Xprinter and Gprinter label models), ZPL (Zebra), EPL and CPCL.
* **Office printers:** anything Android's print service can reach.

Bluetooth Low Energy only printers are not supported. Raw Wi-Fi/LAN printing is not included yet.

A receipt printer can also print a label template as a single image.
